About the EVC

A University of Michigan hub for the future of electric mobility

The Electric Vehicle Center at the University of Michigan advances the technologies, talent, and infrastructure driving electric mobility forward. Founded in 2023 within U-M's College of Engineering, the EVC unites U-M faculty, industry partners, and public agencies around a shared goal.

Strategic pillars

Building on a century of leadership

We build on more than a century of U-M leadership in transportation: educating the next generation of engineers and technicians, innovating alongside the companies designing tomorrow's propulsion systems, and engaging the full ecosystem, from K-12 classrooms to the federal agencies shaping national research priorities.

OUR MISSION

We educate, innovate, and engage to accelerate the future of electric mobility.

Through research, education, and industry collaboration, the Electric Vehicle Center connects students, faculty, and partners to develop the next generation of electric mobility technologies.

OUR VISION

To make Michigan the E-Motor Capital.

By combining world-class research, industry partnerships, and innovation, the EVC aims to establish Michigan as the global leader in electric mobility and transportation technologies.

Our Strategic Pillars

3

Three pillars organize our work — each connected to the others, each anchored in U-M's strengths and our industry partners' priorities.

01

Technology & Innovation

The anchor pillar

The motor of the EVC — where academic rigor meets industry application. U-M Engineering faculty and labs partner with industry co-PIs across eight priority areas: batteries, electric machines & drives, thermal management, end-of-life strategies, charging infrastructure, data & modeling, raw materials & supply chain, and lightweighting.

02

Workforce Development & Education

Cradle to career

Building the talent pipeline from K-12 classrooms to C-suite boardrooms. We inspire young people through K-12 outreach, prepare them through graduate programs and NEXUS certificates, connect them with industry through internships and career expos, and help the existing workforce adapt through community college partnerships, the EV Workforce Portal, and professional development.

03

Facilities & Infrastructure

World-class labs

World-class labs that attract researchers, industry, and talent. The EVC Battery Lab is a signature asset — pilot-line capabilities for pouch cells, cylindrical cells, and module assembly that few universities can match. Battery Lab 3.0, opening at UMTRI, will consolidate and expand that capacity as a comprehensive engineering service for industry and academia. Alongside it, the Lay Automotive Laboratory is converting bays to battery-electric propulsion, and a new bi-directional charging station at Mcity extends our work into vehicle-to-grid research.

Our Team

Our people are consistently identified as our greatest strength. Led by Director Alan Taub and a leadership team spanning technology, education, workforce development, facilities, impact evaluation, and communication, the EVC brings together full-time staff, research faculty, and technology leads from across U-M Engineering and beyond.
