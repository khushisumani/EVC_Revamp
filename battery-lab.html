What We Do

Full-service battery fabrication & testing

From electrode slurry mixing to full cell assembly and characterization — pilot-scale infrastructure without the multi-million dollar build-out.

Cell Fabrication
Pilot-scale electrode coating, calendering, slitting, and cell assembly for cylindrical, pouch, and coin cells.
Electrochemical Testing
Cycling, rate capability, impedance spectroscopy, and calendar aging across a wide range of conditions.
Characterization
SEM, XRD, and tomographic imaging to understand material structure and failure mechanisms.
Thermal Analysis
DSC, TGA, and accelerated rate calorimetry to assess safety and thermal behavior of cells and materials.
Dry Room Access
Controlled low-humidity environment for moisture-sensitive lithium metal and solid-state electrolyte work.
Hands-on Training
Structured lab courses and workforce training for grad students, undergrads, and industry engineers.
Access & Eligibility

Open to researchers
& industry

The Battery Lab operates as a shared-use facility. Whether you're a U-M grad student or a Fortune 500 manufacturer, you can apply for access.

University researchers — U-M faculty, graduate students, and postdocs across any department
Partner institutions — Collaborating universities and community colleges in the EVC network
Industry users — Startups, OEMs, and suppliers seeking pilot-scale R&D without building in-house
Government & national labs — Federal and state agency partners aligned with the EVC mission
